# Scope

## In Scope

- The product includes a public landing page.
- The product includes browser-based encrypted upload and download workflows.
- The product includes a contact page.
- The product includes static site generation and DreamHost deployment support.

## Out of Scope

- The product does not include accounts or authentication.
- The product does not include advanced sharing or collaboration features.
- The product does not include enterprise compliance controls.
- The product does not include server-side decryption.
